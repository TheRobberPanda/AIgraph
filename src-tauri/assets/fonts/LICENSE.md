# Noto Serif

`NotoSerif-Regular.ttf` and `NotoSerif-Bold.ttf` are bundled so that an
exported book renders identically on every machine, with no system font
lookup and nothing fetched at runtime. They cover Latin, Latin Extended-A,
Greek and Cyrillic — which is what makes a Polish or Spanish book come out
with its diacritics intact rather than as empty boxes.
